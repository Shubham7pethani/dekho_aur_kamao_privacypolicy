Privacy Policy for Dekho Aur Kamao

1. Introduction

Welcome to Dekho Aur Kamao ("the App"), owned and operated by SholoTech ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and how we safeguard your data in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and Indian Information Technology laws.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to operate our reward services and payout system:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, and unique Google account identifier. We do not receive, store, or have access to your Google account password.
  • Payment Details (UPI ID): When you request a coin withdrawal, you voluntarily provide your Virtual Payment Address (UPI ID, e.g. username@okhdfcbank). This is collected solely to transfer rewards via direct UPI payout.
  • Transaction & Coin Ledger Data: We maintain logs of rewarded video ads completed, coins credited, coins locked, and payout transaction records for account reconciliation and fraud prevention.
  • Device & Usage Telemetry: Technical data including device model, operating system version, and anonymous advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID) collected through third-party ad SDKs to serve ads and detect abusive or bot activity.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • To authenticate you and maintain your personal reward account.
  • To accurately credit coins earned upon verified video ad completion.
  • To process your withdrawal requests directly to your provided UPI ID.
  • To prevent automated click-fraud, multi-account abuse, and system exploitation.
  • To comply with regulatory obligations and resolve customer support inquiries.
Zero Selling of Personal Data: SholoTech will NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal information, email address, or UPI details to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

4. Third-Party Services & SDK Integrations

To provide video ads and UPI payouts, the App integrates vetted, industry-standard third-party SDKs that process technical and usage data under their respective privacy policies:

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies SF): Serves rewarded video ads. Collects device information, IP address, and ad interaction metrics to deliver relevant advertisements and monitor ad delivery fraud.
  • Google Play Services: Provides secure OAuth 2.0 authentication and core device services.
  • InsForge Cloud Platform: Secure PostgreSQL database and serverless functions infrastructure hosted with enterprise-grade encryption.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement strict technical and organizational safeguards:

  • All network communication between the App, backend servers, and payout APIs is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Database access is guarded by Row-Level Security (RLS), preventing users from accessing or modifying other accounts' coin balances or payment information.
  • Server credentials and private API keys are stored server-side only and are never bundled into the client APK.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • To delete your account, open the in-app Profile tab and tap "Delete Account", or send an email to official.sholotech@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered Google email.
  • Upon receiving your request, your profile, coin balance, and stored UPI ID will be permanently purged from our active databases within 30 days, except for anonymized transaction logs required for statutory financial audits.

7. Children's Privacy

Dekho Aur Kamao is intended solely for users aged 18 and older who are legally capable of entering into binding contracts and receiving UPI payments under Indian law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of the App following any update signifies your acceptance.
